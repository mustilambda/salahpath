📋 How to Install
1
Tap the download button above to download the APK file to your Android phone.
2
Open your Downloads folder and tap the downloaded file named SalahPath.apk.
3
Allow installation — Android will ask to allow installs from unknown sources. Tap Settings → Allow, then go back and install.
4
Open the app, allow location permission, and your prayer times will be detected automatically. You're all set! 🎉
ℹ️

Why "Unknown Sources"? SalahPath is distributed directly (not via Play Store), so Android requires this one-time permission. This is completely safe and normal for apps outside the Play Store.
