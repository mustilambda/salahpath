Features

Everything you need
for your Salah journey

Built with love for Muslims who want to stay consistent with their prayers.

🕐
Auto Prayer Times
Automatically fetches accurate prayer times based on your GPS location using the Aladhan API. Supports 10+ calculation methods.
🔥
Streak Tracking
Build and maintain your prayer streak day by day. Stay motivated with visual progress and milestone celebrations.
🔔
Smart Notifications
Get reminded at prayer time even when the app is closed. Set custom offsets — 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes before each prayer.
🏆
Rewards & Badges
Earn badges and points as you log prayers. Unlock achievements like "Perfect Day", "Devoted Soul", "Golden Path" and more.
🔄
Qaza Prayer Logging
Missed a prayer? Log it as Qaza with a single tap. The app distinguishes between on-time and make-up prayers gracefully.
📖
Daily Inspiration
A rotating collection of Quran verses and Hadith to keep you spiritually motivated throughout the day.
