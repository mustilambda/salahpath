Frequently Asked Questions
Quick answers to common questions.
Is SalahPath free? +
Yes, completely free. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no account required. Download and use it forever.
Why are my prayer times wrong? +
Make sure you've allowed location permission. If times still seem off, try changing the calculation method in Settings — different countries use different methods (e.g. Karachi method for Pakistan, ISNA for North America).
Why does Android say "Unknown Sources"? +
SalahPath is distributed directly as an APK, not through the Google Play Store. Android shows this warning for all apps installed this way — it's safe and completely normal. Just tap "Allow" to install.
Is my data private? +
Yes. All your prayer logs, streak data and settings are stored only on your device. We don't collect or send any personal data to any server.
Will there be an iOS version? +
We're working on it! iOS distribution requires an Apple Developer account. Follow us for updates on when the iOS version will be available.
My notifications aren't working. +
Go to Settings in the app, make sure "Prayer Reminders" is toggled on and the notification permission is granted. On some Android devices, you may also need to disable battery optimization for SalahPath in your phone's settings.
